Below is some data that I have been collecting. Every path that was taken started from the Linkedin job posting which I would then click on the Linkedin profile, from the profile we went to the about section and there was always a link to the company website in the about section. For the most part everything was very similar when it came to finding the "careers" link. At times you would have to search for job openings to see the range of available jobs, others the link itself would take you straight there, but most of the times its the former. Another thing I notices is that the careers tab can for the most part always be found at the bottom of the page. Often times I do get the cookies notification incase that is another issue that we need to address.
